`PreviewCard.Trigger` opens on hover (after `delay`), immediately on focus, and toggles on click. `Content` cancels pending closes on hover and dismisses on hover-away (after `closeDelay`) or `Esc`. `Arrow` centers on the trigger edge like the popover arrow.

## Keyboard interactions

- `Tab` into the trigger opens the card; `Esc` closes it.
- Focus stays put — the card is modeless and never steals focus.
